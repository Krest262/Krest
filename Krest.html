Marathon difficulty index & BQ predictor

Each course is scored 0–100 (higher is harder) from four pillars: grade-weighted elevation, hill placement, climate, and technicality and altitude. Built-in course figures are rough estimates.

How it’s scored

Elevation = (gain + 30% of net change, with descent credit capped at 500 ft and a 25% quad-damage penalty on any descent beyond that) ÷ 25, floored at 0 and capped at 100. Placement = feet of gain in miles 18–26 ÷ 12, capped at 100. Climate = degrees above 52°F plus half the degrees below 45°F, ÷ 36, using an effective temperature that adds 0.6°F per degree of dew point above 55°F when a dew point is given (optional 11th CSV column). Technical = turns (0–10) × 6, plus an altitude bonus above 4,000 ft. Each pillar is capped at 100 and combined using the weights above. BQ chance = 26% minus half the difficulty score, plus up to 6 points for net-downhill courses, kept between 1% and 28%.
